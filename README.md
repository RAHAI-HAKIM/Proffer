# Proffer
Proffer is a mobile app that helps freelancers and independent service providers run their whole client workflow from their phone, from a prospect’s first request to the final payment. It’s built for developers, designers, consultants and other professionals who sell their services directly to clients. Today most of them juggle WhatsApp chats, notes and spreadsheets . As a result, they lose client details, forget follow-ups, and can’t tell who still owes them money.

## Main features

- Lead intake form: the freelancer shares a link, and prospects describe their request through a simple online form, with no app or account needed. Each submission appears in the app as a new lead.
- Leads and clients: one place for contact details, requested services, deadlines, budgets and status, with one-tap calling, WhatsApp or email.
- Quotes: build a quotation from line items with automatic totals, export it as PDF, share it instantly, and track whether it was accepted.
- Payments: record full or partial payments in any currency and always see the outstanding balance.
- Follow-ups: schedule reminders to contact a client, delivered as phone notifications for ease to track.
- Dashboard: today’s follow-ups, quotes awaiting replies, and money still owed, all in one place.
